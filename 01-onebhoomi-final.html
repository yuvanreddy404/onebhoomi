§ 1 — Extraction

It reads like a clerk reads

§ 2 — Workflow

Three schedules before a seal

Schedule Aautomated

Machine checklist

Required fields present, area bounds sane, dates in order, survey formats legal. Obvious failures stop here instead of wasting a clerk's afternoon.

Stage 1 · No human time
Schedule Bclerk

Read & correct

Every extracted field sits in an editor beside its evidence. The clerk fixes OCR slips in place — the record keeps what they approved, not what the machine guessed.

Stage 2 · Human correction
Schedule Cofficer

Approve, or reject with reasons

The officer's approval freezes the record and triggers the seal. A rejection is also kept on file — reason, date, and who said it.

Stage 3 · Decision recorded
§ 3 — The Seal

Unsigned paper is just paper

The approved facts are flattened into canonical JSON — stable, deterministic, no rearranged keys. A SHA-256 digest is taken and signed with the office's own RSA-PSS 2048-bit key, kept on this machine, not in anyone's cloud.

Once sealed, a certified record is immutable. Change one comma and the signature stops matching.

{ "survey_number": "1413", "village": "Mangapet",
  "district": "Mulugu", "stamp_value": "50",
  "document_date": "2019-08-03" …
} → sha256 → RSA-PSS-2048 → seal
